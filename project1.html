Top 5 Most Watched Indian Film Trailers

Verified Box Office Collections Included 😲

1. Salaar Part 1: Ceasefire
Views: 113.2M | 💰 ₹700 Cr

Salaar: Part 1 – Ceasefire is a 2023 Indian Telugu-language epic action thriller directed by Prashanth Neel. Starring Prabhas and Prithviraj Sukumaran, it follows the friendship between a prince and his loyal friend in the dystopian city of Khansaar.

2. KGF Chapter 2
Views: 106.5M | 💰 ₹1200 Cr

KGF: Chapter 2 (2022) is a massive period action film directed by Prashanth Neel. Rocky Bhai (Yash) establishes himself as the king of the Kolar Gold Fields, facing threats from Adheera (Sanjay Dutt) and the government.

3. Pushpa 2: The Rule
Views: 104M | 💰 ₹1700 Cr (Est)

Pushpa 2: The Rule (2024) continues the saga of Pushpa Raj (Allu Arjun). The labourer-turned-syndicate-head faces his arch-rival SP Bhanwar Singh Shekhawat in this high-octane action drama.

4. Adipurush
Views: 85M | 💰 ₹300 Cr

Adipurush (2023) is a mythological action film based on the Ramayana. Starring Prabhas as Raghava and Saif Ali Khan as Lankesh, it retells the epic battle of good versus evil.

5. Animal
Views: 74M | 💰 ₹900 Cr

Animal (2023) is an intense action drama directed by Sandeep Reddy Vanga. It explores the toxic relationship between a son (Ranbir Kapoor) and his father, leading to a brutal path of vengeance.
